Class Types

6 different classes every week

☯

Yoga

Improve flexibility, balance, and mental clarity.

A practice that combines physical postures, breathing techniques, and meditation. Suitable for all levels, from beginners to advanced practitioners. Classes include Vinyasa, Hatha, and Restorative Yoga.
⚡

HIIT

High-intensity intervals for maximum calorie burn.

Short bursts of intense exercise alternated with recovery periods. Burns maximum calories in minimum time. Improves cardiovascular fitness, metabolism, and endurance. Includes bodyweight and equipment-based circuits.
♦

Pilates

Core strength and mindful movement.

Low-impact exercises that strengthen muscles while improving postural alignment and flexibility. Focuses on core strength, controlled movements, and mind-body connection. Uses mat work and specialized equipment.
🚴

Spinning

High-energy cycling workouts.

Indoor cycling classes set to motivating music. Simulates outdoor riding conditions with varied resistance and pace. Low-impact, high-calorie burn. Led by instructors who guide you through sprints, climbs, and intervals.
🥊

Boxing

Build power, speed, and endurance.

Full-body workout combining boxing techniques with fitness training. Includes shadow boxing, bag work, and pad drills. Improves coordination, cardiovascular fitness, and stress relief. All levels welcome, no previous experience required.
